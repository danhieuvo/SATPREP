About this site. SAT Prep offers quick, easy access to SAT-style practice right in your browser, with no need to install College Board's Bluebook testing app. For practice tests that most closely resemble the real SAT, visit College Board's official SAT practice page.

Not affiliated with College Board. SAT® is a trademark registered by the College Board, which is not affiliated with, and does not endorse, this website.

How the questions are made: every question on this site is original. None are copied from College Board, Khan Academy, Princeton Review, or any other test-prep provider. Each one follows the format, content areas, and difficulty range of the digital SAT. Math questions are produced by programs that create new numbers for each problem type and compute and check the answer automatically. Reading and Writing questions are built from original sentences, passages, and data written for this site. People, studies, and figures in tables and charts are illustrative, not real reports.
